Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Who we are

Glut is developed and operated by Thurlabs, based in Switzerland. You can reach us at thurlabs@inboxly.ch for any privacy-related questions.

What we collect and why

Location

Glut uses your device location to display fire spots near you and to calculate distances. Your location is sent to the following third-party services solely to retrieve map data:

  • OpenStreetMap Overpass API — to fetch nearby fire spots
  • swisstopo (Swiss Federal Office of Topography) — to resolve place name searches

Your location is never stored by Thurlabs on any server.


Device identifier

A randomly generated device identifier is created the first time you use the app. It is stored on our servers (Supabase) and associated with photos you upload and any reports you submit. This identifier cannot be used to identify you personally. It exists solely to prevent abuse and to associate your pending photos with your device.


Photos and location metadata

If you choose to upload a photo, the image is stored on our servers. If the photo contains GPS coordinates in its metadata (EXIF), those coordinates are also stored alongside the photo to help verify that the photo was taken at the correct spot. Photo uploads are reviewed before being made publicly visible.


Saved spots

Spots you save as favourites are stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data is never sent to any server.

Where your data is stored

Photos and device identifiers are stored using Supabase, a cloud database provider. Data is stored in the European Union. Supabase complies with GDPR.

How long we keep your data

  • Photos — kept until rejected by a moderator, or until you request deletion.
  • Device identifier — kept as long as associated photos or reports exist. Deleted upon request.

Your rights

Under Swiss data protection law (nDSG) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the data we hold about your device
  • Request deletion of your photos and device identifier
  • Object to processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at thurlabs@inboxly.ch.

Third-party services

  • OpenStreetMap — map data and spot information
  • swisstopo — place name geocoding
  • Supabase — photo storage and database

Glut does not use any advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or tracking frameworks.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
